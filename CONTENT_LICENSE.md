## Adapted worksheets

The interactive Thought Record and Activity Plan adapt worksheet prompts and structure by Robert Fulton:

- [Thought Record](https://www.progresspsychotherapy.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Thought-Record.pdf) (2026), CC BY-SA 4.0.
- [Activity Diary](https://www.progresspsychotherapy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Activity-Diary.pdf) (2025), CC BY-SA 4.0.

The forms have been adapted for interactive use and include revised prompts. They are released under CC BY-SA 4.0 with attribution to Robert Fulton, links to the originals and license, and notice that changes were made. The source catalogue says license terms can vary by tool, so this project links to other worksheets without adapting them unless their individual footer is verified.
